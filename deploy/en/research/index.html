Research

Automatic evaluation of call center response quality

Overview

I research methods that automatically evaluate response quality from operators' response transcripts, using natural language processing and large language models.

Research fields

  • Natural language processing (NLP)
  • Practical applications of large language models (LLMs)

Background

When people evaluate response quality by hand, it takes time and money, and the standard can vary from one evaluator to another.

Purpose

To reduce the cost of manual evaluation and make the evaluation standard more consistent.

Technology and methods

Item Details
Data Operators’ response transcripts
Methods Natural language processing and large language models
Evaluation Automatic evaluation of response quality
